# Corpus Expansion Strategy

Corpus expansion involves adding focused files to teach specific patterns, requiring the selection of at least two extension categories.

## Details

<!-- src:class-4-assignment-brief -->
### In [[Building Custom LLM Assignment]]
- The corpus must be expanded with focused files for at least two extension-eval categories. ([[raw/Class 4 - Assignment Brief#Your Three Choices|§ Your Three Choices]])
- The model must train on the new text, as adding files does not instantly update the model. ([[raw/Class 4 - Assignment Brief#Expand Your Corpus with Files|§ Expand Your Corpus with Files]])
- The comparison between the starter and expanded-corpus experiments must be made in the README. ([[raw/Class 4 - Assignment Brief#README Requirements|§ README Requirements]])
