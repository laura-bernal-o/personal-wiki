The agent's inability to survive longer, despite more training, was due to several factors [S2]:
*   The environment uses `repeat_action_probability=0.25`, which means the game sometimes ignores the agent's chosen move and repeats its previous move, creating a structural death risk [S2].
*   Grayscale preprocessing might obscure the color cue that ghosts turn blue or flashing to signal safety after a power pellet, making the evasion decision harder to learn from pixels alone [S2].
*   Evasion is a more difficult credit-assignment problem than pellet collection, as reacting to moving, chasing ghosts requires more varied experience than what a 5,000-transition replay buffer and infrequent target-network syncing are suited for [S2].

The passages also noted that the evaluation score nearly doubled, suggesting the network got better at collecting points within the same short lifespan, rather than learning to evade ghosts better [S1][S2].
