# Obsidian Wiki Structure

For Obsidian readability, notes should use short, natural filenames (2–6 words) and maintain a clear separation between raw, reviewed, and generated content.

## Details

<!-- src:class-5-personal-wiki-brief -->
### In [[Personal Wiki Assignment]]
- Note filenames should be short, natural, and usually 2–6 words, matching the note's first heading. ([[raw/Class 5 - Personal Wiki Brief#Make the Wiki Readable in Obsidian|§ Make the Wiki Readable in Obsidian]])
- Unchanged originals must be kept in `raw/`, reviewed notes in `wiki/`, and embedded images in `attachments/`. ([[raw/Class 5 - Personal Wiki Brief#Make the Wiki Readable in Obsidian|§ Make the Wiki Readable in Obsidian]])
- The `index.md` file should serve as a human landing page, grouped by topic with short descriptions and links. ([[raw/Class 5 - Personal Wiki Brief#Make the Wiki Readable in Obsidian|§ Make the Wiki Readable in Obsidian]])
