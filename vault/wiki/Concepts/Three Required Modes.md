# Three Required Modes

The system must implement three distinct interaction modes—Chat, Ask, and Search—each with specific functional requirements.

## Details

<!-- src:class-5-personal-wiki-brief -->
### In [[Personal Wiki Assignment]]
- Chat should help brainstorm, draft, plan, and work through ideas, using recent conversation for follow-ups. ([[raw/Class 5 - Personal Wiki Brief#Three Required Modes: Chat, Ask, Search|§ Three Required Modes: Chat, Ask, Search]])
- Ask must treat each question independently of chat history, answer directly in a neutral voice, and show citations. ([[raw/Class 5 - Personal Wiki Brief#Three Required Modes: Chat, Ask, Search|§ Three Required Modes: Chat, Ask, Search]])
- Search must return matching original passages and source locations without generating a synthesized answer. ([[raw/Class 5 - Personal Wiki Brief#Three Required Modes: Chat, Ask, Search|§ Three Required Modes: Chat, Ask, Search]])
