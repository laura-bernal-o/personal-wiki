# Personal Wiki Assignment

This assignment requires building a personal wiki using a local Gemma model and Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) to power three distinct CLI modes: chat, ask, and search.

## Details

<!-- src:class-5-personal-wiki-brief -->
- The system must use local Gemma to organize linked pages and power three distinct CLI modes: chat, ask, and search. ([[raw/Class 5 - Personal Wiki Brief#Overview|§ Overview]])
- The 'ask' mode uses the RAG workflow by retrieving relevant passages, including them in a prompt, and having the model answer using that evidence. ([[raw/Class 5 - Personal Wiki Brief|source]])
- The 'search' mode must return matching original passages and source locations without generating a synthesized answer. ([[raw/Class 5 - Personal Wiki Brief#Three Required Modes: Chat, Ask, Search|§ Three Required Modes: Chat, Ask, Search]])
- The 'run your four question tests' requires testing a direct question, a rephrased question, a question connecting two sources, and a question with insufficient evidence. ([[raw/Class 5 - Personal Wiki Brief#Run Your Four Question Tests|§ Run Your Four Question Tests]])
